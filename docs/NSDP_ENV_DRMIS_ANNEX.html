Annex: NSDP Environment Pillar M&E ↔︎ DRMIS

Purpose. Map how the Disaster Risk Management Information System (DRMIS) informs Vanuatu’s NSDP Environment pillar monitoring & evaluation (not substitute for official indicator reporting).

Legend. P = primary / strong · S = secondary / contributory · W = weak / conditional · — = out of scope unless datasets are explicitly added.

Sources (use together)

Document Role
NSDP M&E Framework (July 2017), DSPPAC — Environment pillar tables Official wording of policy objectives, SMART indicators, targets, SDG alignment.
Environment Pillar: Full NSDP Indicator Progress Factsheet (Environment Pillar All objectives.pdf) 63 environment indicators across five goals; baseline / target / current / status; national portal reporting snapshot; critical observations on data gaps.

Crosswalk — goal titles differ between documents. Use indicator IDs (e.g. 3.3.3, 4.2.2) as the stable key. The factsheet groups goals under short names (e.g. ENV 3 Climate Change & Disaster Risk Reduction, ENV 4 Environmental Protection & Restoration). The 2017 framework uses longer chapter titles for ENV 1–5. Note: the factsheet PDF’s heading for “ENV 5” may not match its printed indicator rows—resolve ambiguities against the 2017 framework or the live portal.

Factsheet headline (DRMIS-relevant). Many indicators show no data or stale baselines on the national portal; ~40% reported online vs ~60% “exploring data sources.” Critical observations: (1) current values often still baseline-era; (2) reporting silos—activity exists but not reflected on the portal; (3) verification links empty. DRMIS can help with (2) and (3) via published APIs, integration keys, and traceable dataset publication—not by replacing VNSO/line ministry owners of each indicator.

ENV 3.3.3 (factsheet row). Multi-hazard / risk maps for PDNA — factsheet lists reported progress (verify against the official NSDP tracking portal). DRMIS remains a credible host for national map products and metadata if designated by NDMO/MoCCA.

DRMIS technical hooks (for M&E authors)

DRMIS capability Typical NSDP use
Clusters (e.g. disaster, climate, logistics) + disaster overlay tags Consistent hazard / exposure groupings for reporting narratives
Province & area council geometries + tabular/vector attributes Subnational disaggregation for risk, damage, needs
Dataset types: baseline, estimated damage, aid resources, financial damage PDNA / recovery evidence (ENV 3.3)
Raster / PMTiles / vector (land cover, coastal, drivers) ENV 3.3.3, ENV 4.x spatial products
API + integration keys Reduce reporting silos; departmental pull of approved, published data
Publication status + audit Traceability and verification paths for MIS-derived evidence
